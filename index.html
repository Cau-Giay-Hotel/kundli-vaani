Kundli

Start your kundli with a short form.

A step-by-step form asks for your name, gender, date, time and place of birth; tick "I don't know my exact birth time" if you are unsure.

No email or password: the app gives you a private recovery code instead, and no account to manage.

● Seven short steps, skip what you don't know
AI Astrologer

Ask an AI astrologer anything.

Pick a persona from the expert gallery and ask about career, love or health. Replies are generated by AI from the birth details and questions you share.

Follow-up chips such as "Tell me more" and "What can I do next?" keep the conversation going without retyping.

● Needs an internet connection to reach the AI service
Rashifal

A fresh rashifal every day, by area of life.

Love Life and Family, Health, Career and Money, and Emotions - four short readings a day, each with a one-tap "What does this mean for me today?" question.

● Entertainment purposes only - not professional advice
Tarot

Pick 3 cards, reveal a short reading.

Choose three cards from the deck and reveal a reading for each - meaning, keywords, and the card name in Hindi alongside English.

● A rewarded ad may play before the reveal

FAQ
Do I need to create an account?

No. Kundli Vaani has no email/password sign-up - you get an anonymous recovery code instead, and your birth details stay on your device.

Does the AI chat need internet?

Yes. AI astrologer chat, daily rashifal and tarot readings are generated by a server and need an active internet connection.

Are the readings real astrological advice?

No - Kundli Vaani is for entertainment and self-reflection only. It is not a substitute for professional medical, legal or financial advice.

What languages does it support?

English, Hinglish, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Kannada.

Is Kundli Vaani free?

Yes, free to download and use, supported by ads.
